Chess Openings

Here are three openings you can try:

  • Italian Game: develop your pieces and aim at the center.
  • Queen's Gambit: challenge your opponent for control of the center.
  • Sicilian Defence: a popular response to White's first move, 1. e4.

Italian Game

1. e4  e5
2. Nf3 Nc6
3. Bc4 Bc5

Try to control the center and bring out your knights and bishops. Then castle to protect your king.
