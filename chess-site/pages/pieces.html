The Chess Pieces

Each player starts with 16 pieces. Here's how they move:

Chess pieces and their moves
PieceHow it movesValue
KingOne square in any directionPriceless
QueenAny distance, straight or diagonal9
RookAny distance, straight5
BishopAny distance, diagonal3
KnightIn an L-shape; can jump over pieces3
PawnForward one square, or two from its starting square; captures diagonally1

Good to know

  • The knight is the only piece that can jump over others.
  • A pawn that reaches the other side can become a queen or another piece.
