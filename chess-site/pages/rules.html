How to Play Chess

Set up the board

  1. Put the board so each player has a light square on the right.
  2. Place the rooks in the corners, then the knights and bishops beside them.
  3. Put the queen on the square that matches her color. Put the king on the remaining square.
  4. Line up the pawns in front of the other pieces.
  5. White moves first. Players then take turns.

Three useful terms

  • Check: the king is under attack.
  • Checkmate: the king is under attack and cannot escape. The game is over.
  • Stalemate: a player has no legal move, but their king is not in check. The game is a draw.
